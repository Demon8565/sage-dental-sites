Our Story

Care that reaches
beyond the chair.

SAGE Dental Clinic provides comprehensive dental care across two locations in Kumasi — Abuakwa and Adiebeba-Ahodwo — offering everything from routine cleaning and fillings to orthodontics, root canal treatment, and full tooth replacement.

What sets SAGE apart is a genuine commitment to community health. Alongside in-clinic care, the practice runs regular dental screening programs — bringing checkups and education directly to people who might not otherwise visit a clinic. It's a small addition to a service list, but it says something bigger about how the team thinks about care: not just treating what walks through the door, but going out to meet the need.

What Guides Us

Three things we don't compromise on.

Community First

Dental care shouldn't stop at the clinic door. Our outreach and screening programs bring checkups directly into schools, churches, and community groups across Kumasi.

Full-Service Care

From a routine cleaning to orthodontics and oral surgery, patients don't need to be referred elsewhere for the care they need most.

Two Locations, One Standard

Whether you visit Abuakwa or Adiebeba-Ahodwo, you get the same attentive, consistent care from a team that knows the neighborhood.
