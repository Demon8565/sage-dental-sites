Community Outreach

Screenings where people already are.

Good dental care shouldn't depend on being able to walk into a clinic. SAGE Dental runs regular dental screening programs across Kumasi — bringing checkups, education, and early detection directly into the community.

Arrange an Outreach Visit
  • Free or low-cost dental screening
  • Oral hygiene education for families
  • Early detection & referral to clinic care
  • Open to schools, churches & community groups
